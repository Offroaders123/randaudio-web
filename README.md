# randaudio-web

Creating music programmatically with PCM audio data!

I am really curious about writing music with math now. Hope that makes sense. It's not for being "smart", but for using those technical concepts creatively and artistically. It's meant to express complexity and intricacies.

I want to make my own sound library, with programmed sounds. I will make them with code, and then mix and master them in Logic. The idea is to look into how PCM audio works, then make sounds based on that. I can then use my creative ears to make it musical.

[Background on PCM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulse-code_modulation)
